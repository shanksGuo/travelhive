## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Paris

### 🏙️ 1 Day in Paris – Iconic Highlights

**Theme**: First Steps in the City of Light

- **Eiffel Tower**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Metro Line 6, Bir-Hakeim Station  
  *Tip*: Book tickets in advance to skip the lines.  
  *Photo Tip*: Best light at sunrise.

- **Louvre Museum**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Metro Line 1, Palais Royal – Musée du Louvre Station  
  *Tip*: Visit early in the morning to avoid crowds.  
  *Link*: [louvre.fr](https://www.louvre.fr)

- **Notre-Dame Cathedral**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Metro Line 4, Cité Station  
  *Tip*: Check opening hours, as renovations may be ongoing.

- **Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur Basilica**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Metro Line 2, Anvers Station, then funicular  
  *Tip*: Explore the picturesque alleys and street artists.

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Paris

- **Metro, RER, Bus, Tram**: Extensive and efficient public transport system.  
  - *t+ Ticket*: €2.50 for a single journey.  
  - *Paris Visite Pass*: 1 to 5-day unlimited travel pass.  
  - *Navigo Easy*: Rechargeable card suitable for visitors.

- **Recommended Apps**:  
  - [Bonjour RATP](https://www.ratp.fr/en/visite-paris/bonjour-ratp): Official schedules and maps.  
  - [Citymapper](https://citymapper.com/paris): Great for real-time navigation.

- **Bike/Scooter Rentals**:  
  - [Vélib'](https://www.velib-metropole.fr): Public bike-sharing service.  
  - [Lime](https://www.li.me/en), [Dott](https://ridedott.com): Electric scooters.

- **Rideshare/Taxi**:  
  - Uber is widely available. Traditional taxis can be hailed or reserved via G7 or LeCab apps.

⚠️ Beware of pickpockets, especially in tourist areas and on crowded trains.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Paris

- **By Air**:
  - *Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)*: Take RER B to city center (~€10.30, 35–45 min).
  - *Orly Airport (ORY)*: Use Tram 7 or Orlyval + RER B combo.

- **By Train**:
  - *Gare du Nord*: Eurostar and northern routes.
  - *Gare de Lyon*: Trains to the south and Switzerland.

🧳 Tip: Reserve long-distance trains early via [SNCF Connect](https://www.sncf-connect.com).

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: Say "Bonjour" or "Bonsoir" when entering any shop or café.
- **Tipping**: Included in bill (service compris), but rounding up or €1–2 is appreciated.
- **Dress Code**: Casual-chic; neat attire is appreciated, especially at restaurants or churches.
- **Religious Sites**: Cover shoulders and knees. Be silent and respectful.
- **Scams**: Avoid petition-signing scams and "gold ring" trick near tourist hotspots.
- **Siestas & Closures**: Many smaller shops close midday or Sundays.

⚠️ Avoid Châtelet station at night if traveling alone – known for late-night loitering.
