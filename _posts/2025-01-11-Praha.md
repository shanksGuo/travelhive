## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Prague

### 🏰 1 Day in Prague – Iconic Highlights

**Theme**: A Day of Historic Wonders

- **Prague Castle & St. Vitus Cathedral**  
  *Duration*: 2–3 hours  
  *Transport*: Tram 22 to Pražský hrad  
  *Tip*: Arrive early to avoid crowds.  
  *Link*: [Prague Castle](https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors)

- **Charles Bridge (Karlův most)**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk from the castle  
  *Tip*: Best experienced at dawn to avoid crowds.  
  *Link*: [Charles Bridge](https://www.amazingczechia.com/sights/charles-bridge/)

- **Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk from Charles Bridge  
  *Tip*: Watch the clock's hourly show.  
  *Link*: [Old Town Square](https://www.amazingczechia.com/sights/old-town-square-prague/)

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Lokál Dlouhááá** – Traditional Czech cuisine, Old Town, €€  
  [lokal-dlouha.ambi.cz](https://lokal-dlouha.ambi.cz/en/)

- **Café Savoy** – Elegant café with Czech and French dishes, Malá Strana, €€  
  [cafesavoy.ambi.cz](https://cafesavoy.ambi.cz/en/)

### 🖼️ 3 Days in Prague – Culture and Charm

**Day 1**: Historic Core

- **Jewish Quarter (Josefov)**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk from Old Town  
  *Tip*: Visit the Old Jewish Cemetery and synagogues.  
  *Link*: [Jewish Museum](https://www.jewishmuseum.cz/en/)

- **Municipal House (Obecní dům)**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk from Josefov  
  *Tip*: Explore Art Nouveau architecture and Smetana Hall.  
  *Link*: [Municipal House](https://www.obecnidum.cz/en/)

**Day 2**: Lesser Town & Vltava River

- **Petrin Hill & Observation Tower**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Funicular from Újezd  
  *Tip*: Offers panoramic city views.  
  *Link*: [Petrin Tower](https://www.prague.eu/en/object/places/93/petrin-lookout-tower-petrinska-rozhledna)

- **Kampa Island & Lennon Wall**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk from Petrin  
  *Tip*: Enjoy art installations and riverside views.

**Day 3**: Modern Prague

- **DOX Centre for Contemporary Art**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Tram 6 or 12 to Ortenovo náměstí  
  *Tip*: Explore modern art exhibitions.  
  *Link*: [DOX Centre](https://www.dox.cz/en)

- **Letná Park**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk from DOX  
  *Tip*: Offers scenic views and a relaxed atmosphere.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Eska** – Modern Czech cuisine, Karlín, €€€  
  [eska.ambi.cz](https://eska.ambi.cz/en/)

- **Kantýna** – Meat-centric dishes and Czech beer, New Town, €€  
  [kantyna.ambi.cz](https://kantyna.ambi.cz/en/)

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Prague

- **Public Transport**:  
  - **Metro**: 3 lines (A, B, C) covering the city.  
  - **Trams**: Extensive network, including night trams.  
  - **Buses**: Serve areas not covered by metro or trams.  
  - **Funicular**: Connects Újezd to Petrin Hill.

- **Tickets and Fares (2025)**:  
  - **30-minute ticket**: 30 CZK  
  - **90-minute ticket**: 40 CZK  
  - **24-hour ticket**: 120 CZK  
  - **72-hour ticket**: 330 CZK  
  [PID Tickets and Fare](https://pid.cz/en/tickets-and-fare/)

- **Apps for Navigation**:  
  - [PID Lítačka](https://pidlitacka.cz/) – Official transport app.  
  - [Citymapper](https://citymapper.com/prague) – Real-time route planning.

- **Rideshares and Taxis**:  
  - **Uber**, **Bolt**, and **Liftago** operate in Prague.  
  - Official taxis have a yellow roof light and company name.

- **Bike and Scooter Rentals**:  
  - **Rekola** and **Nextbike** offer bike-sharing services.  
  - **Lime** and **Bolt** provide electric scooters.

⚠️ **Transit Warnings**: Validate your ticket upon boarding to avoid fines. Be cautious of pickpockets, especially on crowded trams and metro lines.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Prague

- **Airports**:  
  - **Václav Havel Airport Prague (PRG)**:  
    - **Bus 119**: Connects to Metro Line A at Nádraží Veleslavín.  
    - **Airport Express (AE) Bus**: Direct to Main Train Station (Hlavní nádraží).  
    - **Taxi**: Approximately 700–900 CZK to city center.

- **Train Stations**:  
  - **Hlavní nádraží**: Main international and domestic services.  
  - **Masarykovo nádraží** and **Smíchovské nádraží**: Serve regional destinations.

**Local Tips**:  
- Purchase tickets in advance for international trains.  
- Validate all transport tickets before boarding.

## 🏨 Where to Stay in Prague

### 1. **Old Town (Staré Město)** – For First-Time Visitors  
- **Pros**: Central location, close to major attractions.  
- **Cons**: Higher accommodation costs.  
- **Hotel**: [Hotel Kings Court](https://www.hotelkingscourt.cz/) – Luxury hotel with historic charm.

### 2. **Malá Strana (Lesser Town)** – For Romantic Getaways  
- **Pros**: Picturesque streets, near Prague Castle.  
- **Cons**: Can be hilly and less nightlife.  
- **Hotel**: [Alchymist Grand Hotel & Spa](https://www.alchymist.cz/) – Boutique hotel with opulent decor.

### 3. **Vinohrady** – For a Local Experience  
- **Pros**: Residential area with parks and cafes.  
- **Cons**: Slightly away from tourist hotspots.  
- **Hotel**: [Hotel Anna](https://www.hotelanna.cz/) – Cozy hotel in a quiet neighborhood.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Prague

**Must-Try Dishes**:

- **Svíčková na smetaně**: Marinated beef with creamy sauce and dumplings.  
- **Vepřo knedlo zelo**: Roast pork with sauerkraut and dumplings.  
- **Trdelník**: Sweet pastry rolled in sugar and nuts.  
- **Smažený sýr**: Fried cheese, often served with tartar sauce.

**Where to Try**:

- **Lokál Dlouhááá** – Traditional Czech dishes in a lively setting.  
  [lokal-dlouha.ambi.cz](https://lokal-dlouha.ambi.cz/en/)

- **U Kroka** – Homestyle Czech cooking near Vyšehrad.  
  [ukroka.cz](https://www.ukroka.cz/)

- **Eska** – Modern interpretations of Czech classics.  
  [eska.ambi.cz](https://eska.ambi.cz/en/)

**Food-Centric Neighborhoods**:

- **Karlín**: Known for its trendy restaurants and cafes.  
- **Holešovice**: Emerging area with diverse dining options.

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: A firm handshake is customary.  
- **Tipping**: 10% is standard in restaurants.  
- **Language**: Czech is the official language; English is widely spoken in tourist areas.  
- **Dress Code**: Casual attire is acceptable; dress modestly when visiting religious sites.

**Common Scams**:

- **Overpriced Taxi Rides**: Use official taxi apps or stands.  
- **Currency Exchange**: Avoid street exchangers; use official exchange offices.

**Seasonal Rhythms**:

- **Spring (March–May)**: Pleasant weather and blooming flowers.  
- **Summer (June–August)**: Festivals and outdoor events; book accommodations early.  
- **Autumn (September–November)**: Mild temperatures and fewer tourists.  
- **Winter (December–February)**: Christmas markets and snow-covered streets.
