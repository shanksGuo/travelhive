## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Kraków

### 🏰 1 Day in Kraków – Historic Essentials

**Theme**: Dive into the heart of Kraków's Old Town and royal heritage.

- **Main Market Square (Rynek Główny)**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Europe's largest medieval square; don't miss St. Mary's Basilica and the hourly trumpet call.  
  *Link*: [Main Market Square](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/top-things-to-do-in-krakow)

- **Wawel Castle & Cathedral**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: 15-minute walk from Rynek Główny  
  *Tip*: Explore royal chambers and the cathedral's crypts.  
  *Link*: [Wawel Castle](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/top-things-to-do-in-krakow)

- **Kazimierz District**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: 20-minute walk or tram  
  *Tip*: Discover the Jewish Quarter's synagogues and vibrant street art.  
  *Link*: [Kazimierz District](https://www.inyourpocket.com/krakow/krakow-kazimierz-the-former-jewish-district_70207f)

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

### 🖼️ 3 Days in Kraków – Culture and Charm

**Day 1**: Old Town & Royal Route

- **St. Florian's Gate & Barbican**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Start at the medieval gate and stroll the Royal Route.

- **Rynek Underground Museum**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Under Main Market Square  
  *Tip*: Explore Kraków's history beneath the square.

  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Tram to Zabłocie

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Kraków

- **Public Transport**:  
  - **Trams and Buses**: Extensive network covering the city.  
  - **Tickets**:  
    - 20-minute ticket: 4.00 PLN  
    - 60-minute ticket: 6.00 PLN  
    - 24-hour ticket: 15.00 PLN  
    - 72-hour ticket: 36.00 PLN  
    - 7-day ticket: 56.00 PLN  
    [MPK Kraków](https://www.mpk.krakow.pl/en/tickets/types-of-tickets/)

- **Apps for Navigation**:  
  - [Jakdojade](https://jakdojade.pl/krakow) – Real-time public transport schedules.  
  - [Uber](https://www.uber.com/pl/en/) and [Bolt](https://bolt.eu/en/) – Ride-hailing services.

- **Bike and Scooter Rentals**:  
  - **Wavelo**: Bike-sharing system.  
  - **Lime** and **Tier**: Electric scooters available via app.

⚠️ **Transit Warnings**: Validate your ticket upon boarding to avoid fines.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Kraków

  - **John Paul II International Airport Kraków-Balice (KRK)**:  
    - **Train**: 20-minute ride to Kraków Główny station.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Kraków

**Must-Try Dishes**:

- **Pierogi**: Dumplings filled with meat, cheese, or fruit.  
- **Żurek**: Sour rye soup with sausage and egg.  
- **Obwarzanek**: Traditional braided bread rings sold by street vendors.  
- **Zapiekanka**: Baguette topped with mushrooms, cheese, and sauces.

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: A firm handshake is customary.  
- **Tipping**: 10% is standard in restaurants.  
- **Dress Code**: Modest attire when visiting churches.  
- **Public Drinking**: Prohibited; fines may apply.

**Common Scams**:

- **Currency Exchange**: Use official exchange offices; avoid street exchangers.  
- **Overpriced Taxis**: Use registered taxi services or ride-hailing apps.

**Seasonal Rhythms**:

- **Spring (April–May)**: Mild weather and blooming parks.  
- **Summer (June–August)**: Festivals and outdoor events.  
- **Autumn (September–October)**: Fewer tourists and colorful foliage.  
- **Winter (November–February)**: Christmas markets and snow-covered streets.
