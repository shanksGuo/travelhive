## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Nice

### 🏖️ 1 Day in Nice – Coastal Charm & Culture

**Theme**: Seaside strolls, historic alleys, and Riviera flair

- **Promenade des Anglais**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Start your day with a walk along this iconic 7km seafront, lined with palm trees and Belle Époque architecture.

- **Vieux Nice (Old Town)**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Wander narrow, winding streets filled with colorful buildings, local boutiques, and the famous Cours Saleya market.

- **Castle Hill (Colline du Château)**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk or take the free elevator from Quai des États-Unis  
  *Tip*: Best panoramic views over the Bay of Angels. Ideal for sunset photos.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Chez Acchiardo** – Classic Niçoise cuisine, family-run, in the Old Town, €€  
  [chezacchiardo.com](http://www.chezacchiardo.com/)

- **Fenocchio** – Iconic gelato shop with unique flavors like lavender and tomato-basil, €  
  [fenocchio.fr](https://www.fenocchio.fr/)

### 🎨 3 Days in Nice – Art, Nature & Riviera Living

**Day 1**: Seaside & Old Town  
- Follow the 1-day itinerary above.

**Day 2**: Art & Cimiez District

- **Musée Matisse**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Bus #5 or #33  
  *Tip*: Set in a 17th-century villa, showcasing one of the world’s largest collections of Matisse’s work.  
  [musee-matisse-nice.org](https://www.musee-matisse-nice.org/)

- **Monastère de Cimiez & Gardens**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk from Musée Matisse  
  *Tip*: Peaceful cloisters and manicured gardens with views.

**Day 3**: Markets & Parks

- **Cours Saleya Market**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Visit in the morning for fresh flowers, produce, and socca (chickpea flatbread).

- **Parc Phoenix**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Tram Line 2 to Parc Phoenix  
  *Tip*: Family-friendly park with tropical greenhouses and animals.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **La Merenda** – Tiny, no-reservation gem offering rustic Niçoise dishes, €€  
  [No official website – cash only]

- **Le Safari** – Local favorite in Cours Saleya for seafood and Provençal fare, €€  
  [lesafari.fr](http://www.lesafari.fr/)

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Nice

- **Public Transport**:  
  - Operated by **Lignes d’Azur**  
  - **Trams & Buses** cover most of the city  
  - Tickets: €1.70 for a 74-minute pass; 10-trip card: €10  
  - Night buses available  
  [lignesdazur.com](https://www.lignesdazur.com/)

- **Apps for Navigation**:  
  - [Lignes d'Azur App](https://www.lignesdazur.com/fr/application-mobile/146)  
  - [Citymapper](https://citymapper.com/nice)

- **Bike & Scooter Rentals**:  
  - **Vélo Bleu** – Public bike-sharing stations throughout the city  
  - **Lime**, **Dott** – Electric scooters via mobile apps

⚠️ **Tips**: Validate your ticket before boarding; keep it for spot checks.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Nice

- **Nice Côte d’Azur Airport (NCE)**  
  - **Tram Line 2**: Direct to city center (€1.70, ~20 mins)  
  - **Bus 12 or 98**: Alternative to reach central stops  
  - **Taxi/Uber**: ~€30–40 to Old Town

- **Train Stations**:  
  - **Nice-Ville**: Main station, connects to Monaco, Cannes, Marseille  
  - **Nice-Riquier**: Serves local TER trains

**Tip**: Book regional trains early for cheaper seats, especially in summer.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Nice

### Must-Try Dishes:

- **Socca** – Crispy chickpea flatbread, served hot with pepper  
- **Salade Niçoise** – Tuna, anchovies, olives, and local veggies  
- **Pissaladière** – Onion tart with anchovies and olives  
- **Ratatouille** – Stewed vegetable dish from Provence  
- **Pan Bagnat** – Niçoise salad stuffed in bread

### Where to Try:

- **Chez Pipo** – Best-known place for socca, €  
  [chezpipo.fr](http://www.chezpipo.fr/)

- **Olive & Artichaut** – Elegant local bistro, €€  
  [olive-et-artichaut.com](https://www.olive-et-artichaut.com)

- **Boulangerie Jeannot** – For quick pan bagnat or pissaladière, €  
  [Maison Jeannot](https://www.facebook.com/boulangeriejeannot)

- **Vieux Nice**: Local markets, street eats, small bistros

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: Say “Bonjour” when entering shops or restaurants  
- **Tipping**: Service included, but rounding up or 5–10% appreciated  
- **Siesta Hours**: Many small shops close between 1–3 PM  
- **Water**: Tap water is safe to drink  
- **Sunday Closures**: Most shops and groceries closed on Sundays

⚠️ **Common Scams**: Watch for pickpockets in Old Town and on busy trams. Use official taxis or app-based rides.

📅 **Events**:  
- **Nice Carnival** (Feb): One of Europe’s biggest – colorful floats & flower battles  
- **Nice Jazz Festival** (July): International artists at Théâtre de Verdure  
- **Christmas Markets** (Dec): Place Masséna and Jardin Albert 1er
