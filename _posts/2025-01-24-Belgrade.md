## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Belgrade

### 🏛️ 1 Day in Belgrade – Historic Core & River Views

**Theme**: Fortress views, bohemian charm, and Serbian soul

- **Kalemegdan Fortress & Park**  
  *Duration*: 1.5–2 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk from city center  
  *Tip*: Explore the ancient fortress, enjoy panoramic views of the confluence of the Sava and Danube rivers, and visit the Military Museum.

- **Knez Mihailova Street**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Stroll along this bustling pedestrian street lined with shops, cafes, and historic buildings.

- **Skadarlija Bohemian Quarter**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Experience the bohemian atmosphere with cobblestone streets, traditional restaurants, and live music.

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Belgrade

- **Public Transport**:  
  - **Buses, Trams, Trolleybuses**: Operated by GSP Beograd, covering the entire city.  
  - **Tickets**: Purchase a BusPlus card at kiosks and load with credit. Validate upon boarding.  
  - **Night Lines**: Limited routes operating after midnight.

- **Apps for Navigation**:  
  - [Moovit](https://moovitapp.com/) – Real-time public transport updates.  
  - [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/) – Reliable for route planning.

- **Taxis and Ride-Sharing**:  
  - **Taxis**: Use official taxi companies like Pink Taxi or Beo Taxi.  
  - **Ride-Sharing**: Apps like CarGo and Yandex Go are popular alternatives.

- **Bike and Scooter Rentals**:  
  - **Nextbike** – Public bike-sharing system with stations across the city.  
  - **Lime** – Electric scooter rentals via mobile app.

⚠️ **Transit Tips**: Always validate your ticket to avoid fines. Be cautious of unlicensed taxis; use official stands or apps.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Belgrade

- **Airports**:  
  - **Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG)**:  
    - **Bus Line 72**: Connects the airport to Zeleni Venac in the city center.  
    - **Minibus A1**: Direct service to Slavija Square.  
    - **Taxi**: Fixed rates available at the airport taxi info desk.

- **Train Stations**:  
  - **Belgrade Centre (Prokop)**: Main railway station for domestic and international routes.

- **Bus Stations**:  
  - **Belgrade Main Bus Station**: Central hub for intercity and international buses.

**Local Tips**: For airport transfers, consider pre-booking a shuttle or using official taxi services to ensure fixed pricing.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Belgrade

**Must-Try Dishes**:

- **Ćevapi**: Grilled minced meat sausages, often served with flatbread and onions.  
- **Pljeskavica**: Serbian-style hamburger, typically larger and spiced.  
- **Sarma**: Cabbage rolls stuffed with minced meat and rice.  
- **Ajvar**: Roasted red pepper relish, served as a condiment.  
- **Kajmak**: Creamy dairy product, similar to clotted cream.  
- **Rakija**: Fruit brandy, commonly made from plums or apricots.

**Where to Try**:

- **Restoran Proleće** – Traditional Serbian dishes in a cozy setting.  
- **Zavičaj** – Rustic restaurant offering hearty local meals.  
- **Dva Jelena** – Historic eatery in Skadarlija with live music.

**Food-Centric Neighborhoods**:

- **Skadarlija**: Known for its traditional restaurants and bohemian vibe.  
- **Dorćol**: Offers a mix of trendy cafes and classic eateries.

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: A firm handshake is customary; use "Zdravo" (Hello) or "Dobar dan" (Good day).  
- **Tipping**: 10% is standard in restaurants and cafes.  
- **Language**: Serbian is the official language; English is widely understood in tourist areas.  
- **Currency**: Serbian Dinar (RSD); credit cards are accepted in most places.

**Common Scams**:

- **Unlicensed Taxis**: Always use official taxi services or ride-sharing apps.  
- **Currency Exchange**: Use official exchange offices or banks to avoid unfavorable rates.

**Seasonal Rhythms**:

- **Spring (March–May)**: Mild temperatures, ideal for sightseeing.  
- **Summer (June–August)**: Hot weather, vibrant nightlife, and festivals.  
- **Autumn (September–November)**: Pleasant climate, fewer tourists.  
- **Winter (December–February)**: Cold temperatures, festive markets.

**Local Holidays**:

- **Belgrade Beer Fest**: Annual music and beer festival held in August.  
- **Belgrade Music Festival (BEMUS)**: Classical music event in October.

🪧 **Warning Zones**:

- **Nightlife Areas**: Exercise caution in crowded clubs and bars; keep an eye on personal belongings.  
- **Public Transport**: Be aware of pickpockets, especially on buses and trams.
