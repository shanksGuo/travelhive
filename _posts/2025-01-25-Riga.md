## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Riga

### 🏛️ 1 Day in Riga – Old Town & River Views

**Theme**: Medieval charm, market culture, and skyline vistas

- **Old Town (Vecrīga)**  
  *Duration*: 2–3 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Wander cobblestone streets to see the House of the Blackheads, St. Peter’s Church, and Riga Cathedral.

- **Riga Central Market**  
  *Duration*: 1–1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Explore Europe's largest market, housed in former Zeppelin hangars, offering local produce and delicacies. 

- **St. Peter’s Church Tower**  
  *Duration*: 30–45 minutes  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Take the elevator to the top for panoramic views of the city and the Daugava River.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Lido Alus Sēta** – Traditional Latvian dishes in a rustic setting, €€  
  [lido.lv](https://www.lido.lv/en/)

- **Folkklubs Ala Pagrabs** – Cozy tavern offering hearty meals and local beers, €€  
  [folkklubs.lv](https://www.folkklubs.lv/)

### 🎨 3 Days in Riga – Culture, Architecture & Local Life

**Day 1**: Old Town & Central Market  
- Follow the 1-day itinerary above.

**Day 2**: Art Nouveau & Museums

- **Art Nouveau District (Alberta iela & Elizabetes iela)**  
  *Duration*: 1–2 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk or tram  
  *Tip*: Admire over 800 Art Nouveau buildings, the largest collection in the world. 

- **Art Nouveau Museum**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Explore the restored apartment showcasing early 20th-century interiors.

- **Museum of the Occupation of Latvia**  
  *Duration*: 1–1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Learn about Latvia's history during Soviet and Nazi occupations. 

**Day 3**: Parks & Local Neighborhoods

- **Bastejkalna Park & Freedom Monument**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Stroll through the park and visit the iconic Freedom Monument.

- **Kalnciema Quarter**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Tram or bus  
  *Tip*: Visit on Saturdays for the open-air market featuring local crafts and food.

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Riga

- **Public Transport**:  
  - **Modes**: Trams, trolleybuses, and buses operate from 5:30 AM to 11:30 PM.   
  - **Tickets**:  
    - Single ride: €1.15 (pre-purchased), €2.00 (onboard)  
    - 24-hour pass: €5.00  
    - 3-day pass: €10.00  
    - 5-day pass: €15.00  
    - Tickets can be purchased at kiosks, ticket machines, or via the "Rīgas satiksme" mobile app. 

- **Apps for Navigation**:  
  - [Moovit](https://moovitapp.com/) – Real-time public transport updates.  
  - [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/) – Reliable for route planning.

- **Taxis and Ride-Sharing**:  
  - **Taxis**: Use official taxi services; avoid unlicensed cabs.  
  - **Ride-Sharing**: Apps like Bolt and Yandex Go are popular alternatives.

- **Bike and Scooter Rentals**:  
  - **Nextbike** – Public bike-sharing system with stations across the city.  
  - **Bolt** – Electric scooter rentals via mobile app.

⚠️ **Transit Tips**: Always validate your ticket upon boarding to avoid fines.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Riga

- **Riga International Airport (RIX)**  
  - **Bus 22**: Connects the airport to the city center (approx. 30 minutes).  
  - **Minibus 322**: Another option for reaching the city center.  
  - **Taxi**: Official taxis available; ensure the meter is running or agree on a fare beforehand.

- **Train Stations**:  
  - **Riga Central Station**: Main hub for domestic and international routes.

- **Bus Stations**:  
  - **Riga International Coach Terminal**: Serves regional and international bus lines.

**Local Tips**: For airport transfers, consider pre-booking a shuttle or using official taxi services to ensure fixed pricing.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Riga

**Must-Try Dishes**:

- **Grey Peas with Bacon (Pelēkie zirņi ar speķi)**: Latvia’s national comfort food.   
- **Cold Beet Soup (Aukstā zupa)**: Refreshing summer soup made with beets and kefir.  
- **Potato Pancakes (Kartupeļu pankūkas)**: Crispy pancakes often served with sour cream.  
- **Smoked Fish**: Especially popular are smoked sprats and eels.  
- **Rye Bread (Rupjmaize)**: Dense, dark bread, a staple in Latvian cuisine.  
- **Riga Black Balsam**: Traditional herbal liqueur, often mixed with blackcurrant juice.

**Where to Try**:

- **Milda** – Offers traditional Latvian cuisine with a modern twist.  
- **Lido** – Chain of restaurants serving hearty Latvian dishes in a buffet style.  
- **Rozengrāls** – Medieval-themed restaurant located in a historic cellar.

**Food-Centric Neighborhoods**:

- **Old Town (Vecrīga)**: Numerous restaurants and cafes offering local and international cuisine.  
- **Central Market Area**: Fresh produce, meats, and traditional Latvian foods.

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Greetings**: A friendly "Sveiki" (Hello) is customary.  
- **Tipping**: 10% is standard in restaurants and cafes.  
- **Language**: Latvian is the official language; English is widely understood in tourist areas.  
- **Currency**: Euro (€); credit cards are accepted in most places.

**Common Scams**:

- **Unlicensed Taxis**: Always use official taxi services or ride-sharing apps.  
- **Currency Exchange**: Use official exchange offices or banks to avoid unfavorable rates.

**Seasonal Rhythms**:

- **Spring (March–May)**: Mild temperatures, ideal for sightseeing.  
- **Summer (June–August)**: Warm weather, vibrant festivals, and longer days.  
- **Autumn (September–November)**: Pleasant climate, fewer tourists.  
- **Winter (December–February)**: Cold temperatures, festive markets.

**Local Holidays**:

- **Midsummer Festival (Jāņi)**: Celebrated on June 23–24 with bonfires and traditional songs.  
- **Latvian Independence Day**: November 18, marked by parades and fireworks.

🪧 **Warning Zones**:

- **Nightlife Areas**: Exercise caution in crowded clubs and bars; keep an eye on personal belongings.  
- **Public Transport**: Be aware of pickpockets, especially on buses and trams.
