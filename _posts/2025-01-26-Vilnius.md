## 🗺️ Realistic Itineraries for Exploring Vilnius

### 🏛️ 1 Day in Vilnius – Historic Core & Cultural Highlights

**Theme**: Baroque beauty, medieval charm, and artistic flair

- **Vilnius Old Town (Senamiestis)**  
  *Duration*: 2–3 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Explore one of Europe’s largest Baroque old towns, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, including Town Hall Square, Pilies Street, and hidden courtyards.

- **Gediminas’ Tower**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Climb for panoramic views over the city and the Neris River. Small museum at the top.  
  [link](https://www.lnm.lt/en/gediminas-castle-tower/)

- **Užupis District**  
  *Duration*: 1–1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Explore the self-proclaimed artistic republic with street art, quirky cafes, and its own constitution posted on a wall in 23 languages.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Etno Dvaras** – Traditional Lithuanian fare like cepelinai and cold beet soup, Old Town, €€  
  [etnodvaras.lt](https://www.etnodvaras.lt/)

- **Lokys** – Medieval-style dining with game meats in a historic cellar, €€  
  [lokys.lt](https://www.lokys.lt/)

### 🎨 3 Days in Vilnius – History, Art & Green Spaces

**Day 1**: Explore Old Town & Užupis  
- Follow the 1-day itinerary above.

**Day 2**: Museums & Churches

- **Vilnius Cathedral & Bell Tower**  
  *Duration*: 1 hour  
  *Transport*: Walk  
  *Tip*: Climb the bell tower for skyline views and tour the crypts.  
  [vilniauskatedra.lt](https://www.katedra.lt/)

- **Palace of the Grand Dukes of Lithuania**  
  *Duration*: 2 hours  
  *Transport*: Adjacent to the cathedral  
  *Tip*: Explore the historical reconstructions and exhibitions.  
  [valdovurumai.lt](https://www.valdovurumai.lt/)

- **MO Museum**  
  *Duration*: 1.5 hours  
  *Transport*: Walk or trolleybus  
  *Tip*: Contemporary art in a striking modernist building by Daniel Libeskind.  
  [mo.lt](https://mo.lt/en/)

**Day 3**: Day Trip or Green Spaces

- **Vilnius TV Tower or Belmontas Park**  
  *Duration*: 2–3 hours  
  *Transport*: Bus or taxi  
  *Tip*: TV Tower has a rotating café with views; Belmontas offers scenic walking trails along waterfalls.

**🍽️ Meal Suggestions**:

- **Senoji Trobelė** – Rustic Lithuanian dishes like pork knuckle and herring, €€  
  [senoji.lt](https://www.senoji.lt/)

- **Džiaugsmas** – Creative modern Lithuanian cuisine by chef Martynas Praškevičius, €€€  
  [dziaugsmas.lt](https://dziaugsmas.lt/)

## 🚇 Local Transportation in Vilnius

- **Public Transport**:  
  - Buses and trolleybuses operate 5 AM – midnight.  
  - Tickets:  
    - Single ride (mobile): €0.65  
    - Onboard: €1.00  
    - 1-day pass: €5.00  
    - 3-day pass: €8.00  
  - Buy tickets via Trafi app or kiosks.

- **Apps**:  
  - [Trafi](https://www.trafi.com/) – Best app for real-time transport info.  
  - [Bolt](https://bolt.eu/) – For taxis and scooters.

- **Taxis/Rideshare**:  
  - Use **Bolt** or **etaksi** to avoid overcharging.  
  - Standard taxi fare from airport to center: ~€10–15.

- **Scooters & Bikes**:  
  - Bolt and CityBee offer rentable electric scooters.  
  - Cycling is possible in parks and along the river.

⚠️ **Tips**: Validate your ticket or e-ticket before boarding.

## 🚄✈️ Arriving and Departing Vilnius

- **Vilnius International Airport (VNO)**:  
  - **Bus 88/3G** or train to city center, ~15–20 mins  
  - **Taxi/Bolt**: ~€10–15  
  [vilnius-airport.lt](https://www.vilnius-airport.lt/en/)

- **Train Station**:  
  - Vilnius Railway Station connects to Kaunas, Klaipėda, and Warsaw.  
  - Located ~10 minutes’ walk from Old Town.

- **Bus Station**:  
  - Right next to the train station. Buses run to Trakai, Riga, Tallinn, and Warsaw.

## 🍜 Food & Drink in Vilnius

**Must-Try Dishes**:

- **Cepelinai** – Potato dumplings filled with pork, topped with sour cream and bacon  
- **Šaltibarščiai** – Cold beetroot soup with kefir and boiled egg  
- **Kibinai** – Pastry from the Karaim minority, filled with lamb or vegetables  
- **Gira** – Fermented bread drink similar to kvass  
- **Lithuanian Mead** – Honey-based alcoholic beverage  
- **Žemaitiškas Blynai** – Stuffed potato pancakes

**Where to Try**:

- **Aline Leičiai** – Cozy spot with local beer and traditional menu  
  [leineciai.lt](https://www.leiciai.lt/)

- **Gaspar’s** – Modern European-Lithuanian fusion by an Indian-Lithuanian chef  
  [gaspars.lt](https://www.gaspars.lt/)

- **Bernelių Užeiga** – Popular for hearty traditional food and wooden decor  
  [berneliu.lt](https://www.berneliu.lt/)

**Food-Centric Areas**:

- **Old Town**: Historic restaurants, terraces  
- **Užupis**: Artistic cafés and local bistros  
- **Station District (Stotis)**: Edgier food halls like Halės Turgus

## 🙏 Cultural Tips & Local Advice

- **Language**: Lithuanian is official, but English widely spoken in tourist areas  
- **Currency**: Euro (€); card payments accepted almost everywhere  
- **Tipping**: 10% appreciated, but not mandatory  
- **Greetings**: “Labas” (hi), “Ačiū” (thank you)  
- **Church Etiquette**: Dress modestly in religious sites

**Avoid These Mistakes**:

- Don’t walk in bike lanes – they’re clearly marked.  
- Don’t confuse Lithuania with other Baltic nations; locals are proud of their heritage.

🪧 **Safety Tips**:

- Vilnius is safe, but beware of pickpockets on public transport.  
- Stick to marked taxis or ride apps to avoid scams.

📅 **Festivals to Note**:

- **Kaziuko Mugė (St. Casimir’s Fair)** – Early March: traditional crafts, music, street food  
- **Vilnius International Film Festival** – March  
- **Capital Days Festival** – September: concerts, street theatre, fireworks
